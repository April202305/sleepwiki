# Section 1.1 Blueprint

## Paragraph 1

- Role: burden context
- Main claim: Sleep-disorder burden must be framed as disease-specific, definition-specific, and region-specific; it is not one pooled prevalence.
- Evidence IDs: Bassetti-2026, van-Straten-2025, Benjafield-2025, Niu-2025, Song-2024
- Contrast or transition: From disease burden to intervention need.
- Forbidden content: No unified global sleep-disorder prevalence; no adding or ranking heterogeneous estimates.

## Paragraph 2

- Role: clinical need and guarded bridge to closed-loop intervention
- Main claim: Guideline-backed insomnia treatment establishes a clinical intervention context, while the evidence gap requires closed-loop BCI to be treated as a testable monitoring-intervention coupling rather than proven therapy.
- Evidence IDs: Riemann-2017, Edinger-2021, Schutte-Rodin-2008, Dudysova-2024
- Contrast or transition: Section 1.2 should define how systems move from monitoring to real-time regulation.
- Forbidden content: No claim that CBT-I is unavailable, that current therapies fail generally, or that closed-loop stimulation has demonstrated clinical efficacy.
