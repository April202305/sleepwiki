# Evidence Coverage

## Section 1.1

Covered:

- Disease-specific burden for five European sleep disorders.
- Insomnia prevalence under diagnostic interview, global population estimation, and China meta-analysis contexts.
- China OSA prevalence and global adult RLS prevalence.
- Adult chronic insomnia guideline treatment context for CBT-I.
- Chronic insomnia closed-loop auditory stimulation pilot evidence and limits.

Not covered:

- One unified global prevalence for all sleep disorders.
- Circadian rhythm disorder burden.
- Cross-region economic burden comparison.
- CBT-I real-world access or adherence.
- Closed-loop intervention clinical efficacy or long-term safety.
