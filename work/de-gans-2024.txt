   6. Practice points

    1. EEG-based wearables demonstrate impressive accuracy in sleep staging, competing with the reference standard of PSG, while offering a more
       comfortable and less intrusive alternative.
    2. Potential users ought to select the most user-friendly system according to their personal needs and should consider the costs associated with
       these devices, as they vary significantly




   7. Research agenda

    1. To ensure generalizability of EEG based wearable performance beyond the controlled sleep lab environment, more research is required in
       real-world settings, such as home or other non-clinical settings.
    2. Considering the diverse variety of hardware, data pre-processing, and sleep staging algorithms in the field, future research may concentrate
       on comparative analyses of these components. Such studies would facilitate the identification of the most effective and comparable materials,
       thereby enhancing the quality and reliability of outcomes within this domain.
